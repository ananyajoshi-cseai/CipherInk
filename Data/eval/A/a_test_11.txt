A further knowledge of facts is necessary before I would venture to give a final and definite opinion. By the way, Mr. Baynes, did you find anything remarkable besides this note in your examination of the house?” The detective looked at my friend in a singular way. “There were,” said he, “one or two _very_ remarkable things. Perhaps when I have finished at the police-station you would care to come out and give me your opinion of them.” “I am entirely at your service,” said Sherlock Holmes, ringing the bell. “You will show these gentlemen out, Mrs. Hudson, and kindly send the boy with this telegram. He is to pay a five-shilling reply.” We sat for some time in silence after our visitors had left.
